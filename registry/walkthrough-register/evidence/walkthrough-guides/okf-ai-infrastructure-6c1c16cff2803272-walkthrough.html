4. Assemble a qualified answer

An example answer, limited to this bundle, is:

The federated RAG record describes an emerging approach that keeps documents and indexes distributed. Its evidence base is smaller than that of classical federated learning. Keeping training data decentralised does not itself establish privacy: the learning record warns that updates can still leak information.

Secure aggregation lets a coordinating server obtain a sum without reading individual contributions. Differential privacy describes calibrated noise that bounds the effect of one record on outputs. These records describe different mechanisms; their reference links do not prove that a particular RAG implementation combines them correctly.

The independently found evaluation rubric covers utility, efficiency, robustness, privacy, reproducibility and governance. It treats accuracy without auditability, identity or human oversight as insufficient for government use. The separately retrieved NCSC reference supplies an official source to inspect for security guidance. This bundle cannot establish that the external guidance is current or that a proposed service satisfies it.

The selected records have no current verified event. They remain unverified under the core trust rule even where status is stable. A missing stale_after is not a fresh review or a fabricated expiry date. This corpus does not establish operational effectiveness, deployment approval or a privacy guarantee for a proposed service.

The Explorer journey manifest records the searches, actual result selections, graph clicks and metadata inspection for this question. A browser acceptance receipt must bind that manifest to the exact bundle and Explorer build before those steps count as executed checks.

5. Check the negative case

Ask whether a directed concept-reference path proves that the RAG record is connected to the evaluation or NCSC record. The declared concept-only graph has neither path. The correct response is to keep those independent search results separate, rather than invent a relationship.

Also ask whether decentralisation alone proves that the proposal protects privacy. The cited learning record explicitly prevents that conclusion. Where the bundle cannot establish a claim, the answer must say that the evidence is insufficient and identify what further review is needed.
