AI capability governance — correspondence

Question

In the published composite example, what changes when the agent is given the send action, and which human control remains in place?

Worked answer

The baseline is a Tier 3 example in which the agent classifies correspondence and drafts a suggested reply from approved templates; a caseworker commits the action. In the published send variant, sending is treated as an irreversible effect and the example moves to Tier 4. Its cap remains L1 Draft only and shadow mode is required, so a person still commits the output. The four links in this companion graph are synthetic-fixture interpretations of that composite example, not official mandates.

Limits and negative controls

  • This is an illustrative composite adapted from the pinned public playbook, not an account of a real local service or real correspondence.
  • The bundle records no local appointment, authorisation, approval or permission to send messages.
  • All four graph assertions remain qualified as synthetic-fixture with synthetic authority; they do not become official policy through visualisation.
  • The published example is evidence for what its author described, not evidence that a local authority has adopted the same controls.
