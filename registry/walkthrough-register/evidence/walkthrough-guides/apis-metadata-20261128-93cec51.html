Worked answer

The GOV.UK API Catalogue row titled GOV.UK Notify is associated with Government Digital Service. Its generated Markdown records https://api.notifications.service.gov.uk/ and documentation at https://www.notifications.service.gov.uk/documentation. It says access model unknown, contract status documentation-only, and licence not specified. Its source row last-modified date is 24 August 2020.

A different GOV.UK API Catalogue row, GOVUK Notify, is associated with the Department for Work and Pensions. Its record has no endpoint URL, records access model unknown and contract status undocumented-in-catalogue, and states an ISC licence with source-declared basis. Its source row last-modified date is 23 August 2022. This record-level licence cannot be transferred to the Government Digital Service entry.

The similarly named Government Notify record from Wayfinder is synthetic comparison material. Its .example endpoint, synthetic authority, synthetic-fixture scope and rights-not-specified state do not describe a live UK government service.

Decision boundary: Do not merge the records or infer current availability, security, procurement suitability or licence coverage. Confirm the intended provider and obtain current service documentation, contract and rights evidence before reuse.
