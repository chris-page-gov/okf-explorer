Does OKF 0.3 conformance mean this ONS catalogue is complete, current, or statistically verified?

Worked answer from the core sources

No. The core describes a bounded metadata snapshot across four source lanes, not every ONS dataset, and contains no statistical observations.

It defines no governed stale_after date; current-value and live-execution claims need provider revalidation.

Governance says structural validation and frozen-input integrity do not establish statistical truth, while the standards concept separates OKF 0.3 structure from accuracy, legal compliance and certification.

The conclusion about completeness, currentness and accuracy is an inference from these stated limits.
