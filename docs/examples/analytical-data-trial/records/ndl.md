# National Data Library ageing population record trial

The NDL record lists Warwickshire County Council as publisher and UK Open Government Licence as its licence. Its last metadata update is 12 August 2013. It lists an S3 CSV distribution and a separate XLS resource.

The captured CSV URL and its HTTPS form both returned 403 AccessDenied on 11 November 2026. The original S3 distribution remains unassessed. Its bytes have no established equivalence with the CSV retrieved through the WCC publisher record.

The trial proposes a companion manifest for the separately acquired WCC distribution. The companion is a local conversion with a passed offline check and an unresolved rights declaration. The catalogue record has its own identity, metadata and access route.
