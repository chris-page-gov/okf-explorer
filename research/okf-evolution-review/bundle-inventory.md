## Important absences and limits

The scan only used tracked files in direct and one-level nested repositories
under `/Users/crpage/repos`. It did not treat `_site`, build output, dependency
trees or untracked documents as separate products. Remote-only branches,
deleted repositories and private conversations may contain additional
history. The JSON evidence preserves the exact selected paths and commit
identities so another reviewer can challenge this classification.
